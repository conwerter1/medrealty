Until September 30, festival organizers in Portugal will be able to apply for Available Event status.

📅 August 22, 2026 MedRealty Editorial theportugalnews.com →

The Affordable Festivals program officially recognizes activities that make culture accessible to all. This is an important initiative for the country’s event industry.

Any venue can participate, ready to implement the necessary solutions for guests with disabilities.
