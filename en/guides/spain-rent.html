Renting property in Spain: residential, seasonal or tourist use?

Start with the purpose of occupation and a specific address, not an advertised nightly rate. This checklist helps prepare questions for an owner, manager and lawyer. It does not confirm permission to let an individual property.

MedRealty editorial · Sources checked 2026-10-03

What to know

  1. 1. Establish the contract's purpose
  2. 2. Check the current legal version
  3. 3. Investigate the region and municipality
  4. 4. Compare net cash flow
  5. 5. Before signing or starting activity

1. Establish the contract's purpose

The LAU distinguishes permanent housing needs from other uses, including seasonal letting. Tourist accommodation meeting the relevant conditions is governed separately. A short duration alone does not resolve every question: ask a lawyer to assess the actual purpose and terms, not just the contract's heading.

For your own comparison, describe three scenarios in ordinary language: a family's home, temporary occupation for a specific reason and guest accommodation. Record likely dates, occupants and services. Avoid a financial model that assumes the same agreement is conveniently seasonal for the owner but offers permanent-home protection to the occupier. Resolve that contradiction before comparing projected income.

  • Who will occupy the property, why and for how long in practice?
  • What termination, renewal and expense provisions are proposed?

Sources: BOE: Urban Leases Act, current consolidated version

2. Check the current legal version

As checked on 3 October 2026, BOE records the repeal of Real Decreto-ley 26/2026 by the resolution of 2 October. Reporting its September amendments without the subsequent repeal can mislead. Separately, the consolidated RD 1312/2024 marks national-register articles 8–10 as annulled by court decisions.

Our practical conclusion is that an old guide to obtaining a single national number cannot automatically be treated as current or sufficient. Ask the competent authority and your lawyer to identify the applicable procedure for your dates. Do not infer that every tourist restriction disappeared when particular provisions were annulled; local checks are a separate task.

  • Keep the checking date and applicable official texts.
  • Confirm both an existing permit's status and requirements for a new operator.

Sources: BOE: repeal of Real Decreto-ley 26/2026, 2 October 2026 · BOE: RD 1312/2024 and court-annulment annotations

3. Investigate the region and municipality

Andalusia's official portal provides procedures for tourist-use dwellings and a register search. It is a regional example, not a Spain-wide instruction. Your Europe also explains that EU rules do not make registration compulsory for every short-term property; applicable procedures depend on the territory.

Before purchasing, prepare an address-specific enquiry covering municipality, building type, proposed operator, guest capacity and advertising method. Ask for written answers about permitted use, procedures and documents. Have your lawyer review the owners' community rules and decisions separately. A neighbouring property's operation does not establish that a new listing in your building is permitted, and an advertisement is not a legal opinion.

  • Where can the property's status and restrictions on new activity be checked?
  • Which conditions attach to the dwelling and which to its operator?

Sources: Junta de Andalucía: tourist dwellings and procedures · Your Europe: short-term accommodation rules in the EU · Registradores: buyer guide, basic checks; 2020 edition

4. Compare net cash flow

This is our modelling method, not a market forecast. For residential letting, enter monthly revenue and paid months; for guest use, enter the nightly rate and paid nights. Deduct realistic management, cleaning, utilities, platform costs, maintenance and vacancy. Have taxes calculated separately by an adviser rather than assigning an arbitrary percentage of profit.

Record who handles an evening breakdown, meets guests and answers complaints if you live abroad. Test a scenario in which the guest model is temporarily unavailable. If the purchase only works at full occupancy without repairs, that is a dependency in your spreadsheet, not evidence of a dependable return.

  • Use the same full-year period for revenue and costs.
  • Do not treat a refundable occupier deposit as recurring income.

Sources: BOE: Urban Leases Act, current consolidated version

5. Before signing or starting activity

Collect a written assessment of the proposed regime, address-specific confirmations, a management agreement, a draft occupier contract and an expense budget. Keep three statuses in your comparison: confirmed, needs checking and unacceptable. Do not pay for an investment scenario whose essential permission is still only assumed.

This informational guide reflects accessible official publications on the stated date. It is not a property-specific legality certificate or personalised legal advice. Consult an independent Spanish lawyer and tax adviser before launching a letting activity. Repeat the checks when the rules, operator or proposed use change.

Sources: BOE: Urban Leases Act, current consolidated version
