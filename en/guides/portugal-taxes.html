Buying property in Portugal: build a complete cost budget

The seller's price is only the starting point. A useful acquisition budget records amounts, calculation assumptions and payment dates. This guide explains preparation, not your personal tax liability. Confirm rates, reliefs and the consequences of your tax status for the actual transaction with a qualified Portuguese professional.

MedRealty editorial · Sources checked 2026-10-03

What to know

  1. Sort costs by payment stage
  2. Verify the IMT base first
  3. Treat relief as a verified condition
  4. Separate stamp duty and completion services
  5. Budget beyond the transaction

Sort costs by payment stage

Create three groups: preparation, completion and the first year of ownership. Obtain quotes for independent review, a survey, translation and financing; then separate the price, taxes and completion work from maintenance, insurance, repairs and borrowing costs. Record the payee, currency, evidence and conditions for changes beside every item. This is an editorial planning method, not an official fee schedule.

An unknown charge is not zero. Mark it as awaiting a quote and identify who will confirm it. When converting currency, account separately for the transfer fee and actual exchange rate. If the contract credits a deposit towards the price, do not add it again to the full price. Agree the payment timetable before accepting binding obligations.

Sources: Ministry of Justice — Casa Pronta operations and charges

Verify the IMT base first

Article 12 of the IMT Code sets a general rule using the higher of the contractual value and the property's taxable value, VPT. Do not substitute VPT for the seller's price or assume it creates a lower tax base. Ask for both figures, the right being acquired and your ownership share in the written calculation.

Our practical suggestion is to reconcile the inputs with the property documents and draft contract before calculating percentages. Resolve mismatches first. A share purchase, multiple properties or another unusual structure should not inherit a standard apartment calculation without review. Date the calculation and retain its assumptions so that a later price change can be traced to the correct version.

Sources: Portuguese Tax Authority — IMT Code, Article 12: taxable base

Treat relief as a verified condition

Article 17 distinguishes acquisition categories and includes separate provisions concerning nonresidents. A table for a permanent main home is not automatically appropriate for every foreign buyer. Citizenship, a plan to relocate and applicable tax residence are not interchangeable inputs. Ask a local tax adviser which current provision governs your circumstances.

Request four answers: the legal basis, the evidence needed, when each condition must be met and the consequence if it is not. Keep a no-relief scenario until entitlement is confirmed. A general government guide or an old calculator should not override the current Code and transaction-specific advice. This article deliberately does not publish one universal IMT rate or promise an exemption.

Sources: Portuguese Tax Authority — IMT Code, Article 17: acquisition categories and rates

Separate stamp duty and completion services

The Tax Authority identifies stamp duty on the acquisition separately; borrowing can introduce additional tax costs. Payment or applicable exemption evidence is required at completion. Request an itemised calculation so acquisition duty is not hidden inside bank charges or counted twice.

Casa Pronta combines completion and registration operations, with service charges depending on the operations involved. Compare quotes using the same scope: included work, registration actions and separately payable costs. Translation, a survey and independent representation belong in their own budget lines. A convenient completion route should not be assumed to include a building condition assessment or personalised tax advice. Obtain written confirmation of what each provider actually undertakes.

Sources: Portuguese Tax Authority — buying a home, IMT and stamp duty · Ministry of Justice — Casa Pronta operations and charges

Budget beyond the transaction

IMI is an ownership cost, with municipalities setting the annual applicable rate under the Code's rules. Verify the municipality, year and property circumstances rather than copying the seller's last bill. Ask your adviser whether additional property taxation or any relief needs separate assessment; this guide does not decide its applicability.

Request building charges and planned works, and obtain insurance and servicing quotes. Base repair allowances on inspection rather than flattering photographs. Before signing, reconcile the confirmed budget with available funds and payment dates. This is general information, not an expert-reviewed assessment of your transaction. An appropriately qualified local professional should confirm the final calculation and any legal or tax obligations.

Sources: Portuguese Tax Authority — IMI Code, Article 112: municipal annual rates
