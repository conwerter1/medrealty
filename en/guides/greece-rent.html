Check before you decide

Long-term renting in Greece: agreement, deposit and costs

Ask for the draft lease and a list of charges before transferring money. This checklist is for ordinary long-term residential rentals and helps compare the total cost.

MedRealty editorial · Sources checked 2026-11-11

What to know

  1. Before paying: lease and deposit
  2. Budget for the first month
  3. Check the declaration in myAADE

Before paying: lease and deposit

Ask who is entitled to rent out the home. Check the address, dates, key handover and payment terms. Record the early termination and deposit refund terms in writing.

  • Check the recipient and any grounds for deductions. Keep a record of each payment.
  • At handover, note existing defects, inventory and meter readings. Retain the lease and handover record.

Sources: AADE: decision A.1068/2025 on electronic lease declarations

Budget for the first month

Add the first rent payment, deposit, any agent’s fee and one-time connection charges. List utilities, heating and shared building charges separately. Count the deposit as cash needed at move-in even if it can be returned under the lease.

  • Ask for sample summer and winter bills. Compare the same items across properties.

Sources: AADE: decision A.1068/2025 on electronic lease declarations

Check the declaration in myAADE

When the landlord submits the lease declaration, the tenant receives a myAADE notice. Under decision A.1068/2025, the tenant has 30 days from the sending of that notice to accept or reject the details. The declaration is treated as accepted if no action is taken.

  • Check the address, parties, term and rent against the signed lease. If you reject the declaration, explain the mismatch in the application.

Sources: AADE: decision A.1068/2025 on electronic lease declarations

General editorial information, not individual legal or tax advice. Requirements depend on the property, address and buyer's circumstances; confirm them with an independent local professional before committing.
