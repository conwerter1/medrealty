Editorial policy

How MedRealty selects, verifies and updates its coverage.

Selection

We publish stories related to property markets, ownership rules, taxation, rental, residency and investment risk.

Sources and facts

Every story links to its original source. Numbers, dates, currencies and names are preserved when preparing summaries and translations.

Corrections

When a source changes a material fact or we identify an error, the story is corrected. Correction requests can be sent through @MedRealtyNews.

Independence

MedRealty does not sell property, receive developer commissions or publish undisclosed advertising.
