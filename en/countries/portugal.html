What to know

Start with the intended use

A permanent home, a second home and a rental property call for different questions. Write down the purpose, holding period and spending ceiling before viewing listings. These guides help you prepare for professional advice; they do not promise returns, mortgage approval or residence rights.

Do not apply one buyer's circumstances to another. The government buying guide is specifically addressed to EU citizens. Check your own tax status and transaction conditions with an appropriately qualified local professional.

  • Compare Lisbon and Porto using the same property specification and budget definition.
  • Separate the purchase price from completion and first-year expenses.
  • Request documents before making financial commitments.

Sources: gov.pt — official property buying and selling guide for EU citizens

Use three separate checks

The permanent property certificate reports current registrations and pending registration requests. It is a starting point for legal review, not an engineering report. Our editorial framework keeps market comparisons, expenses and property checks separate.

  • Market: record the source, reference period and geographical boundary.
  • Budget: obtain a written calculation of taxes and commercial charges.
  • Property: compare legal status, actual layout and anticipated works.
