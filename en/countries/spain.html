What to know

  1. Choose a purpose first
  2. Budget and title are separate checks
  3. Rental rules require an address
  4. Use guides and news differently

Choose a purpose first

Prepare three lists: daily requirements, cash available before completion and a use you could accept without rental income. Visit the neighbourhood at different times, test essential journeys and request the actual building costs. This is an editorial comparison method, not a city ranking or a property-price forecast.

  • For relocation: everyday journeys, services and physical condition.
  • For holidays: costs while absent and maintenance arrangements.
  • For letting: establish lawful use at the address before modelling income.

Sources: Registradores: Property Registry information

Budget and title are separate checks

Agencia Tributaria distinguishes the tax treatment of a developer's first delivery from later sales. Do not transfer another property's budget to yours. A nota simple helps identify registered ownership and encumbrances; physical condition and permitted use require additional checks.

  • Request a calculation for the autonomous community and transaction date.
  • Match the advertisement to registry documents.
  • Keep a reserve for costs after completion.

Sources: Agencia Tributaria: VAT or transfer tax when buying housing · Registradores: Property Registry information

Rental rules require an address

A national headline cannot establish the legality of a tourist-rental business. Andalusia, for example, publishes regional procedures for viviendas de uso turístico; municipal requirements need checking separately. An online listing does not replace confirmation that your proposed use is permitted.

  • Identify the rental category and competent authority.
  • Check building, municipal and regional requirements.
  • Model vacancy and expenses, not revenue alone.

Sources: Junta de Andalucía: tourist-use dwellings
